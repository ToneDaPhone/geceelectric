About us

A licensed crew that treats every job like it's their own home.

GEC Electric is a family owned and operated electrical contractor, based right here in Colorado, USA, dedicated to helping homeowners and businesses power, light, and protect their spaces safely. We take pride in delivering high-quality workmanship with a level of care and professionalism that's hard to find today.

We specialize in a wide range of electrical services — from panel upgrades and new construction wiring to custom lighting design, EV charger installs, and commercial and industrial work. Whether you're updating a breaker panel or wiring an entire tenant space, our goal is simple: to provide safe, code-compliant work you can trust.

What sets us apart is our commitment to honesty, clear communication, and doing the job right the first time. Every project, big or small, gets the same attention to detail and the same licensed standard of work.
